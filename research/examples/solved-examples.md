## Arithmetic mean (F-STP-mean, grade 7, easy)

**Problem:** Six quiz scores are 7, 9, 6, 8, 10 and 8. Find the mean.

**Given:** Scores: 7, 9, 6, 8, 10, 8

**Required:** Mean score

**Formula:** `\bar{x}=\frac{\sum x_i}{n}`

**Substitution:** `\bar{x}=\frac{7+9+6+8+10+8}{6}`

**Calculation:**
- `Sum = 48`
- `\bar{x}=48/6=8`

**Answer:** Mean = 8 points

**Explanation:** The sum of all values divided by the number of values.
